Health knowledge should reach everyone.

Thrive Health is a community health awareness initiative. We turn trusted medical guidance into simple, practical steps and bring free screenings to where people live.

What we do

01

Educate

Plain-language guides, workshops and campaigns on the conditions that affect our communities most.

02

Screen

Free community check-ups for blood pressure, blood sugar and more, run by trained volunteers.

03

Support

Referrals to local clinics and follow-up, so no one is left alone with a result.

The values behind every guide and every screening.

Trust
Evidence-based and reviewed by health professionals.
Access
Free, local and easy to understand.
Dignity
Private, respectful and without judgement.
Community
Led by the people we serve.
